# Resources & Further Reading

This guide curates high-quality, well-established resources for each learning track. Resources are grouped by type: formal training, professional guidance from regulatory bodies and standards organisations, and practical tools you'll encounter in the field.

---

## Enterprise Track

### Training & Courses

[Berkeley Law GenAI for Legal Profession](https://executive.law.berkeley.edu/programs/generative-ai-for-the-legal-profession/) — Executive-level programme addressing AI governance, ethics, and practical implementation in legal departments and firms. Suitable for in-house counsel and firm leaders.

[ICAEW GenAI Training](https://www.icaew.com/technical/technology/artificial-intelligence) — Chartered accountancy-focused training covering audit, assurance, and governance implications. Directly relevant to financial services organisations.

### Professional Guidance

[ABA Formal Opinion 512](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/centers_commissions/center-for-innovation/artificial-intelligence/) — American Bar Association's landmark guidance on lawyers' ethical obligations when using AI. Essential reading for legal compliance and professional conduct.

[Law Society of England and Wales GenAI Essentials](https://www.lawsociety.org.uk/topics/ai-and-lawtech/generative-ai-the-essentials) — Regulatory guidance from UK's solicitor governing body covering competence, confidentiality, and client communication requirements.

[FRC Guidance on AI in Audit](https://www.icaew.com/insights/viewpoints-on-the-news/2025/jul-2025/frc-publishes-landmark-guidance-on-the-uses-of-ai-for-audit) — Fresh, 2025 guidance from the Financial Reporting Council on integrating AI tools whilst maintaining audit quality and independence.

[NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) — Comprehensive framework for enterprise-wide AI governance, risk assessment, and mitigation strategies. Cross-jurisdictional applicability.

### Tools & Platforms

[Harvey AI](https://www.harvey.ai/) — Specialist legal AI platform built on Claude, supporting document review, legal research, and contract analysis across multiple jurisdictions.

[Clio Legal Practice Management](https://www.clio.com/) — Cloud-based legal practice management system increasingly integrated with AI capabilities for time tracking, billing, and document management.

## Developer Track

### Training & Courses

[Model Context Protocol Documentation](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/) — Official documentation for MCP, the emerging standard for connecting AI agents to external systems and data sources. Foundation for agentic development.

[Claude Code Best Practices](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/claude-code-best-practices) — Anthropic's guidance on integrating Claude Code into development workflows, including prompt engineering and error handling patterns.

[Claude Agent SDK Guide](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-agents-with-the-claude-agent-sdk) — Step-by-step introduction to building autonomous agents with Claude, covering planning, tool use, and multi-step reasoning.

[GitHub Copilot Code Review](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/agents/code-review) — Best practices for using agentic code review, including automation patterns and quality gates.

### Professional Guidance

[Anthropic 2026 Agentic Coding Trends Report](https://resources.anthropic.com/hubfs/2026%20Agentic%20Coding%20Trends%20Report.pdf) — Forward-looking analysis of how agents are reshaping development practices, with case studies from leading organisations.

### Tools & Platforms

[Cursor AI IDE](https://cursor.com/) — Modern code editor with built-in AI capabilities and agentic features for intelligent code generation and refactoring.

[GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot) — Industry-standard AI pair programmer, now extending into agent-like capabilities for entire development workflows.

[Claude Code](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code) — Command-line interface for integrating Claude into development environments and CI/CD pipelines.

## Academic Track

### Training & Courses

[Stanford Library AI for Research Guide](https://guides.library.stanford.edu/ai_research/how_to_use_AI) — Practical guide on integrating AI tools into research workflows, from literature review to data analysis, with ethical considerations.

[UKRIO Embracing AI with Integrity](https://ukrio.org/ukrio-resources/embracing-ai-with-integrity/) — UK Research Integrity Office resource addressing responsible AI use in academic research, authorship, and publication.

### Professional Guidance

[UKRI GenAI Policy for Applications](https://www.ukri.org/publications/generative-artificial-intelligence-in-application-and-assessment-policy/) — UK Research and Innovation official policy governing AI use in research applications and assessment, mandatory for grant applicants.

[NIH Policy on AI in Research Applications](https://grants.nih.gov/news-events/nih-extramural-nexus-news/2025/07/apply-responsibly-policy-on-ai-use-in-nih-research-applications-and-limiting-submissions-per-pi) — US National Institutes of Health guidance on AI disclosure and responsible use in funded research (2025 update).

[Nature Portfolio AI Editorial Policies](https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/ai) — Guidance from leading publisher on AI use in manuscript preparation, reproducibility, and disclosure requirements.

[Wiley AI Guidelines](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/publish/book/resources/ai-guidelines/) — Publisher guidance on AI use in academic publishing, covering authorship, methodology, and transparency.

### Tools & Platforms

[Elicit](https://elicit.com/) — AI research assistant for literature synthesis, helping researchers identify themes and extract insights from academic papers at scale.

[Consensus](https://consensus.app/) — Search engine for peer-reviewed research with AI-powered summary and findings extraction from multiple papers simultaneously.

[Scite Smart Citations](https://scite.ai/) — Citation tracking tool that uses AI to understand whether papers support or contradict cited claims, improving research integrity.

[Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) — AI-powered academic search engine identifying important papers and connections across domains automatically.

[ResearchRabbit](https://www.researchrabbit.ai) — Literature discovery tool using AI to find relevant papers, track research directions, and build topic-specific reading lists.
