# Working With AI: A Practical Course

Welcome to a course designed for the age of agentic AI. This isn't about learning to code — it's about developing the mental models, thinking patterns, and practical habits needed to collaborate effectively with AI systems.

## Who This Course Is For

Everyone shares the same core; you then choose one or more specialised tracks:

| Audience | Your Context | What You'll Gain |
|----------|--------------|------------------|
| **Enterprise Professionals** | Using AI at work, navigating compliance | Governance patterns, approved channels, trust architecture |
| **Developers** | Building with and for AI | Interface design, MCP tools, architectural judgment |
| **Academic Researchers** | Literature, writing, research management | Citation workflows, reproducible methods, AI peer review |
| **Creative Professionals** | Writing, design, content creation | Voice preservation, creative iteration, attribution |
| **Operations & Knowledge Workers** | Documents, data, meetings, admin | Workflows, data wrangling, process documentation |
| **Educators & Trainers** | Teaching, curriculum design, assessment | AI-resistant assessment, personalisation, teaching AI literacy |
| **Leaders & Decision-Makers** | AI strategy, team building, vendor decisions | Strategic assessment, risk management, build-vs-buy |

## The Core Insight

A heuristic that shapes this course:

> **Most routine production work — drafting, boilerplate, first passes — has become dramatically less valuable.** AI handles it in seconds.
>
> **But a small set of skills has become far more valuable:** knowing what you want, expressing it precisely, recognising when you got it, and catching the confident mistakes along the way.

This course teaches that small set.

## Course Structure

### Core Modules (Everyone)

1. **How AI Actually Works** — The ten honest facts about language models that everything else depends on
2. **Structured Thinking** — Linearisation, specification, expressing problems clearly
3. **Precision Without Formalism** — Unambiguous thinking in plain language
4. **Understanding AI Systems** — Model, context, tools, memory — and what an "agent" really is
5. **Verification & Quality** — Catching confident mistakes; matching scrutiny to stakes
6. **Iteration & Refinement** — Treating AI collaboration as dialogue; knowing when to restart
7. **The Agentification Shockwave** — How AI spreads across fields, and what that means for yours

### Specialised Tracks (Choose Your Path)

Seven tracks, four modules each — enterprise, developer, academic, creative, operations, education, and leadership. Use the **Choosing Your Track** page after the core, or jump straight in if you already know yours.

## How to Use This Course

**Self-paced:** Work through at your own speed. The sidebar tracks your progress.

**Modular:** Complete the core, then choose your track(s). Tracks can be done in any order.

**Interactive:** Exercises and quizzes throughout. These aren't tests — they're thinking prompts. The exercises are where the learning actually happens; do them with whatever AI tool you use day to day.

**Portable:** This is a folder of files. Take it with you. Edit it. Build on it.

## How This Course Was Made

In the spirit of its own subject matter: this course is written and maintained in collaboration with AI models, with human editorial direction and review. Claims you can act on have been checked; where a figure or example couldn't be verified, we say so rather than letting it stand. If you spot an error, that's the verification module working — please report it.
