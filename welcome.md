# Working With AI: A Practical Course

Welcome to a course designed for the age of agentic AI. This isn't about learning to code in the traditional sense — it's about developing the thinking patterns, mental models, and practical skills needed to collaborate effectively with AI systems.

## Who This Course Is For

This course serves three audiences, with a shared core and specialised tracks:

| Audience | Your Context | What You'll Gain |
|----------|--------------|------------------|
| **Enterprise Professionals** | Using AI at work, navigating compliance | Governance patterns, approved channels, trust architecture |
| **Developers** | Building with and for AI | Interface design, MCP tools, architectural judgment |
| **Academic Researchers** | Literature, writing, research management | Citation workflows, reproducible methods, AI peer review |

## The Core Insight

A heuristic that shapes this course:

> **90% of traditional coding education has become >90% less valuable.** Syntax, boilerplate, framework tutorials — AI handles these trivially now.
>
> **But 10% has become 10x more valuable.** Specification, verification, architectural judgment, understanding what you're asking for and whether you got it.

This course focuses on that 10%.

## Course Structure

### Core Modules (Everyone)

1. **Structured Thinking for AI Collaboration** — Linearisation, specification, the skill of expressing problems clearly
2. **Precision Without Formalism** — Unambiguous thinking in multiple notations
3. **Understanding AI Systems** — Agent vs prompt vs tools vs memory
4. **Verification & Quality** — Testing, debugging, the visual reasoning gap

### Specialised Tracks (Choose Your Path)

- **Enterprise Track** — Trust architecture, approved channels, governance
- **Developer Track** — Affordances, architecture, MCP tool design
- **Academic Track** — Literature management, modular writing, adversarial AI review

## How to Use This Course

**Self-paced:** Work through at your own speed. The sidebar tracks your progress.

**Modular:** Complete the core, then choose your track(s). They can be done in any order.

**Interactive:** Exercises and quizzes throughout. These aren't tests — they're thinking prompts.

**Portable:** This is a folder of files. Take it with you. Edit it. Build on it.
